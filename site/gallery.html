Pets, selfies, stickers — anything with a clear subject works. The tool handles cutout and gridding automatically.

💡

配色技巧

Color Tips

「最多颜色数」越小越省豆省事（新手 8~12 色足够），越大越还原照片。

Lower "max colors" = fewer bead types, faster to sort (8-12 for beginners). Higher = more faithful to the photo.
